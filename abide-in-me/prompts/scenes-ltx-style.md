# Abide in Me — LTX clip prompts (44 shots)

**The rule for every shot:** The light is the life of God: His presence, Spirit, living water, Word and love. Wherever it works, two things must show: the physical signs of life (grey to warm, still to breathing, dry to green, cold to warm, a closed face opening, one becoming many) and, above all, a real emotional turn on a human face (delight, relief, being seen, forgiven or loved, reunion, laughter through tears). It behaves like living water: it wells up inside a person, pools in the chest, overflows out through the hands and tears, flows into the next person, and at the end the many springs become one river. Where the light works, a real emotional turn must show on a face, or the shot is rebuilt.

Each shot shows its eight fields, then the prompt LTX gets: one paragraph plus the AVOID line, sent as is. Settings: 1920×1080, 24 fps (shot 32 at 48 fps), clip 8 seconds or less, prompt enhancer off. Image-to-video shots start from the shot's still; text-to-video shots are described only.

Hebrew letters (John 15:4–5, אנכי הגפן ואתם השריגים · עמדו בי ואני בכם) are drawn into the stills with a proper font; the prompts only make them drift and glow.
