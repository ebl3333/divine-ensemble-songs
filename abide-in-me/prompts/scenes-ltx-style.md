# Abide in Me — LTX clip prompts (all 48 shots)

Album: *God Is About to Show Up* (track 2, reprise, 4:56) · Divine Ensemble · Production: Dazadi Music

Each prompt is one flowing paragraph in present tense: **subject → action → camera → lighting → lens → how the frame ends**. One prompt = one clip; paste the paragraph and its AVOID line together, with that shot's still as the starting picture. Times and shot details are in `../SHOT-LIST.md`.

## Look and camera rules

Every prompt below already contains these lines word for word, so faces and light stay the same from clip to clip.

**Settings:** 1920×1080, 24 frames per second, prompt enhancer off, each clip 8 seconds or less, the scene's still as the starting picture. The two walking clips (W1, W2) are filmed at 48 frames per second so they stay smooth when slowed.

**Yeshua** — same character as "Jesus Savior Redeemer Friend" (character sheet 01):
> Yeshua, a first-century Galilean Jewish man in his early thirties, six feet tall and noticeably taller than those around him, lean and weathered with sun-tanned olive skin, calloused hands, dark brown hair to the nape of his neck, a full dark beard and dark brown eyes, wears a plain undyed cream tunic belted at the waist and a large cream rectangular mantle with dark stripes and knotted tassels at its four corners, with leather sandals.

- Close shots: *The camera stays close and beside him, in profile or three-quarter view and never directly in front of him, resting on the line of his chin and beard, his mouth, and his dark brown eyes as they catch the light.*
- Wide and drone shots: *Seen from a distance, he stays small in the frame and is never turned squarely toward the camera.*
- Outdoors by day: *His mantle is drawn up over his head, softly shadowing his face beneath the cloth.*
- His light: *A faint warm golden light rests around Yeshua, subtle and steady, never a halo or a flash.*
- In ancient and modern scenes: *He wears his same first-century clothing in this later age, and the people around him take him for an ordinary passer-by.*

**The light He gives** (grows only after His touch or a good choice): *A soft warm golden light begins from nothing and slowly grows within her, glowing gently from her chest and face, never flashing.*

**Camera:** never still, always moving in depth; pans travel right to left; push-ins and pull-backs alternate so cuts flow; every move eases in and out. Color stays warm gold; the alley, the billboards, the rain and the empty lobby start cold and are warmed by His light. Fine grain only in the two lamplit intimate shots (29 and 38).
