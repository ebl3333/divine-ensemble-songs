# Abide in Me

Album: *God Is About to Show Up* (track 2, reprise, 4:56) · Divine Ensemble · Production: Dazadi Music

The song is Yeshua's own voice: "abide in Me, and I in you." He is never shown singing; He appears mostly as light, first as glowing Hebrew letters, the living Word, then as light that streams like points of living knowledge into people's minds and builds their spirits from within. The film climbs a "Word became flesh" ladder: dream (the Word) → drawn 2D → sculpted 3D → real flesh → glory. Six neighbors in one apartment building are joined by His light like branches on one vine; one of them, David, turns away, hits bottom and comes home. His own life (wilderness, Gethsemane, the cross, the empty tomb) runs alongside theirs. At the end all are gathered as the Bride and their lights become one vine of light.

**The rule for every shot:** The light is the life of God: His presence, Spirit, living water, Word and love. Wherever it works, two things must show: the physical signs of life (grey to warm, still to breathing, dry to green, cold to warm, a closed face opening, one becoming many) and, above all, a real emotional turn on a human face (delight, relief, being seen, forgiven or loved, reunion, laughter through tears). It behaves like living water: it wells up inside a person, pools in the chest, overflows out through the hands and tears, flows into the next person, and at the end the many springs become one river. Where the light works, a real emotional turn must show on a face, or the shot is rebuilt.
