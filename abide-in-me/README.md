# Abide in Me

Album: *God Is About to Show Up* (track 2, reprise, 4:56) · Divine Ensemble · Production: Dazadi Music

John 15: "I am the vine, you are the branches." Yeshua walks through every generation as an ordinary-looking man carrying a quiet golden light; everyone He touches begins to glow as they do good through ordinary hard work, kindness and charity, and their fruit is the next generation. Three time-streams cut together: His time (Galilee vineyard, Gethsemane, the cross, the empty tomb), ancient days, and today. At the end all of them gather as one people around Him, the Bride, and their lights become one vine of light across the earth.

| Time | Movement |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:41 | The Vine — His touch kindles light across the ages |
| 1:41–2:58 | Determination — the hard choice to do good, resisting the world's pull |
| 2:58–4:06 | Joy — tasting the fruit of labor, rising in His presence |
| 4:06–4:30 | Sacrificial love — the cross, every burden laid on His (no blood) |
| 4:30–4:56 | Resurrection and His coming — gathered as the Bride, the vine of light |
