## Shots 01–15 · the Word and the drawn world (0:00–1:41)

### 01 · 0:00–0:07 · Before the Light

*"I am the vine, you are the branches"* · The Word (dream)

- **Emotion:** Hush. Something is about to be spoken.
- **Scene:** A warm-gold darkness with no floor, no sky and no horizon, deep as the inside of a closed eye.
- **Action:** One point of light breathes in the dark, slowly swelling and dimming like a heartbeat, then opens into a single glowing Hebrew letter, aleph, made of soft living fire.
- **Camera:** Drifts forward through the dark toward the light, weightless, as if carried on breath. **Ends on:** The aleph glows alone and close in the center of the dark.
- **Feeling:** Awe before beginning; the stillness before a voice.
- **Sense:** The faint warmth of a candle seen through closed eyelids; a low hum you feel more than hear.
- **Not to do:** No stars, planets or space imagery; no sudden flash; no readable caption; the light grows from nothing, never pops on.

### 02 · 0:07–0:13 · The Letters Awaken

*"Abide in Me and you will see"* · The Word (dream)

- **Emotion:** Wonder. The Word stirs.
- **Scene:** The same golden darkness, now like still water reflecting a sky that isn't there; dream logic, up and down the same.
- **Action:** Dozens of glowing Hebrew letters rise out of the dark water like embers lifting from a fire, turning slowly, some drifting upward, some falling upward, gathering into loose constellations.
- **Camera:** Floats slowly between the rising letters, gliding right to left as they pass close by the lens. **Ends on:** A wide field of floating golden letters hanging in the dark like stars over water.
- **Feeling:** Like waking inside a dream and knowing it means something.
- **Sense:** Embers rising on warm air; ripples spreading without a sound.
- **Not to do:** No Matrix green or computer-code look; no clean typed text in rows; letters stay soft, alive and gold.

### 03 · 0:13–0:20 · The Vine of Letters

*"A life made whole, a heart that flourishes"* · The Word (dream)

- **Camera:** Slowly tilts up along the growing trunk from its roots of script to its branches. **Ends on:** The vine of light fills the frame, branches reaching outward into the dark.
- **Feeling:** The first breath of creation; the Word becoming something that lives.
- **Sense:** The slow creak of growing wood; warmth rising like sap.
- **Not to do:** No glowing tree-of-life cliché clip art; no symmetrical logo shape; it grows organically, never instantly.
