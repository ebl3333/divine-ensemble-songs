# God Is About to Show Up — Camera Plan

Each clip has one main camera move. Moves are planned shot to shot so that each cut flows into the next one.

## Rules for every clip
1. **One move per clip.** It starts slowly, speeds up and settles at the end. Cuts happen while the camera is easing, never in the middle of a fast move.
2. **Keep the direction across a cut.** When a clip ends moving right, the next one starts moving right. Circling shots always go the same way, clockwise seen from above.
3. **Up and down need a reason.** The camera rises when the story looks to heaven, and it comes down when heaven comes to earth. A rise is never cut straight into a fall unless something on screen motivates it, such as someone looking up or light pouring down.
4. **Matching shapes at the cuts.** Clips cut from face to face, light to light, or sky to sky where possible.
5. **Each picture shows the clip's key moment, not necessarily its first frame.** The clip builds toward it. Glow always starts at nothing and grows from within.
