# God Is About to Show Up — Camera Plan

The camera is never still. Every clip moves in depth, toward or away from the people, and has a gentle sideways drift, so the viewer always feels the space and the distance to the subject. Consecutive clips are coordinated.

## Rules
1. **In and out alternate.** A clip that moves from far to near (ending close) is followed by one that starts near and moves far. Each cut joins close to close or wide to wide, so nothing jumps.
2. **One sideways direction: right to left.** Every circling shot turns right to left, and the next circling shot continues right to left. Push-ins and pull-backs also drift slightly right to left, so the motion carries across cuts.
3. **Close-ups arrive from far.** A clip that ends on a close-up starts wide and travels in.
4. **Glow grows while the camera comes in.** Scenes where the glow is the event are always push-ins: no glow when wide, full glow when close.
5. **Each move eases in and out.** Cuts land on gentle motion, never mid-swing.
6. Natural pauses (the music breaks at 1:05 and 1:58, and the gap before J1) allow two moves the same way in a row.

## Frames
- `stills/zoom/X-wide.png` = wide view of the scene; `stills/X.jpg` = the approved picture; `stills/starts/` = no-glow and other opening frames.
- **In:** wide → picture. **Out:** picture → wide. **Circle:** picture-based, with right-to-left rotation in the prompt.
