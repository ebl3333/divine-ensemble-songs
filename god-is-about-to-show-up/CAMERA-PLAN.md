# God Is About to Show Up — Camera Plan (Ed's directions)

General rules: the camera is never still; it moves in depth so the viewer feels the space and the distance to the subject. Circling shots turn right to left and the next one continues right to left. Glow always grows from nothing. Each move eases in and out so cuts are smooth.
