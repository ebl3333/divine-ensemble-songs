# God Is About to Show Up — Camera Plan

Every clip moves in depth. The camera either **pushes in** toward the people, **pulls back** away from them, or **circles** them, and a circling shot also pushes in or pulls back as it goes (a spiral). Each picture marks the clip's key moment. A push-in ends on the picture; a pull-back starts on it.

## How each clip is built
- **Push in:** first frame = a zoomed-out view of the picture (`stills/zoom/X-wide.png`), last frame = the picture. If the glow is the event, the wide view has no glow and it grows during the push.
- **Pull back:** first frame = the picture, last frame = the zoomed-out view.
- **Circle:** first frame = the picture. The camera circles clockwise (seen from above) while pushing in or pulling back.

## Rules
1. One main move per clip. It starts slow, flows, and eases out, so cuts never land mid-swing.
2. Alternate in and out for rhythm and depth, and keep the direction when the story continues.
3. Circles always go clockwise.
4. Glow always starts at nothing and grows from within.
