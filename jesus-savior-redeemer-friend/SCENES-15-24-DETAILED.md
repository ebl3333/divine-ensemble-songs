# Jesus Savior Redeemer Friend — Scenes 15–24 (Detailed)

## Scene 15 — The Leper Runs Home
- **Subject:** The healed leper running home to his family, overjoyed.
- **Action:** The joyful aftermath of his healing. The man who was an outcast, made clean, runs back toward his village and family. He bursts into his home/village, showing his clean skin, throwing off the rags and hood that marked him. His family sees him and runs to embrace him — the one who couldn't be touched is now held and welcomed back. Tears of joy, disbelief, celebration. The captive set free, returned to belonging.
- **Scene:** A path leading back to his village, then the village/home — modest first-century houses, family members in simple dress. Warm daylight, dusty road behind him, home ahead. Joyful, bright.
- **Camera:** Open with a wide drone shot following him from above as he runs down the road toward the village, dust kicking up, home coming into view; drop lower to a tracking shot running alongside him, catching his face full of disbelief and joy; cut to his family looking up, the moment of recognition; push in as they run together and embrace; close 85mm on faces — tears, laughter, hands touching the skin that was once untouchable; pull back wide as the whole family wraps around him.
- **Lyric:** "Every captive finds redemption / every prisoner takes flight."

## Scene 16 — Garland for the Widow
- **Subject:** The widow of Nain, now joyful, her son alive beside her.
- **Action:** The aftermath of the raising. The mother who was broken with grief is now crowned with joy. Villagers place flowers and garlands on her and her son; mourning clothes give way to celebration. She holds her son's face, laughing through tears. The hired mourners' wailing turns to singing and dancing. Ashes to beauty, sorrow to song.
- **Scene:** Just outside the town gate of Nain — the same spot as the funeral, now transformed: the bier abandoned, flowers and garlands appearing, villagers gathering in joy. Warm late-afternoon golden light.
- **Camera:** Open on the discarded bier and burial cloths, then tilt up to the widow and son embracing; move in as villagers drape garlands; 85mm on her face shifting from grief-worn to radiant; pull back wide as the crowd breaks into song and dance around them.
- **Lyric:** "You give garlands for the ashes / turning mourning into song."

## Scene 17 — Carrying the Mat
- **Subject:** The once-paralyzed man walking, carrying his own mat.
- **Action:** The man lowered through the roof now stands, rolls up the very mat he was carried in on, lifts it to his shoulder, and walks out through the astonished crowd on his own two legs — testing them, almost dancing, praising God. The crowd parts in amazement. The broken made whole.
- **Scene:** Inside and then outside the Capernaum house — the same basalt home, the hole still open in the roof above, the crowd pressed around. He walks out into bright daylight.
- **Camera:** Close on his legs taking the first steps, the mat lifted to his shoulder; follow behind as he walks through the parting crowd toward the door; cut to amazed faces; move out with him into the daylight; 85mm on his joyful face, then wider as he raises his arms.
- **Lyric:** "The broken find their healing."

## Scene 18 — Rest Under the Olive Tree
- **Subject:** Yeshua and a weary, weighed-down person (or small group) finding rest together.
- **Action:** A tired, burdened traveler — or a small group of weary followers — comes to Yeshua and sits with him in the shade. He welcomes them, offers water, speaks gently. The weight visibly lifts; shoulders ease, faces soften into peace. A quiet, tender moment of being cared for ("Come to me, all who are weary").
- **Scene:** Under a large old olive tree on a hillside, dappled shade, the Galilean countryside rolling beyond, soft warm light. Peaceful and still.
- **Camera:** Wide on the olive tree and the figures settling in the shade; slow drift in; 50mm for the group, 85mm close-ups on a weary face relaxing, hands receiving water; hold long and gentle, very little movement — restful.
- **Lyric:** "The weary find their rest."

## Scene 19 — The Village Dance
- **Subject:** Villagers who were grieving, now dancing together in celebration.
- **Action:** A whole village breaks into joyful dance — people who had been mourning now spinning, clapping, children laughing, musicians playing. Circles of dancers, arms linked, feet stamping in the dust. Pure communal joy. Those who wept now dance.
- **Scene:** A village square/courtyard in the evening, oil lamps and torches glowing warm, tables of food, garlands. Festive, warm, alive.
- **Camera:** Open wide/drone over the whole dancing village from above, circles turning; drop into the crowd handheld, moving with the dancers; 85mm on laughing faces, clapping hands, stamping feet; cut between wide joy and intimate faces; end lifting back up on the drone over the celebration.
- **Lyric:** "The mourning find their dancing."

## Scene 20 — Peter Steps Out (Bridge)
- **Subject:** Peter stepping out of the boat onto the water toward Yeshua.
- **Action:** The bridge moment — Peter, afraid but trusting, swings his legs over the side of the boat and steps onto the water. He stands, walks a few steps toward Yeshua, eyes locked on him, the other disciples watching in astonishment from the boat. Fear overcome by faith — stepping out onto the impossible.
- **Scene:** The Sea of Galilee at night — the same windy, choppy water as Scene 4 (not a heavy storm, no rain), slivers of moonlight on the water. Yeshua standing out on the water ahead, the boat behind.
- **Camera:** Close on Peter's foot touching the water's surface; move up to his face — fear and determination; track alongside as he takes his first steps; reverse to Yeshua waiting with open hand; wide to show Peter small on the vast water between boat and Yeshua; 85mm on the disciples' amazed faces in the boat.
- **Lyric:** "The fearful walk in faith." (bridge)

## Scene 21 — She Stands Tall
- **Subject:** The healed bleeding woman, now standing tall and restored.
- **Action:** The woman who pushed low through the crowd to touch the hem — once weak, ashamed, bent down — now stands upright, strong and radiant, head lifted. Where she once hid, she stands openly in the light, restored and unashamed. The fallen lifted to victory.
- **Scene:** The marketplace / an open area in warm daylight, the crowd around now parting respectfully. She stands in the light, dignified. **Clothing:** her character sheet shows a dark navy mantle — keep her in that navy mantle here (not a red head covering) for consistency across the video.
- **Camera:** Start low, looking up at her as she rises to full height; push in on her face, strong and at peace; a slow circling move around her standing tall; pull back to show her upright and whole in the open, the crowd around her.
- **Lyric:** "The fallen stand victorious."

## Scene 22 — Bartimaeus Follows
- **Subject:** Bartimaeus, now seeing, following Yeshua down the road.
- **Action:** The once-blind beggar, newly healed, joins the crowd following Yeshua on the road out of Jericho. He walks close behind him, drinking in everything his new eyes can see — faces, sky, the road — praising God with every step, free at last from darkness and from begging in the dust. The prisoner set free.
- **Scene:** The road leaving Jericho, palm trees, the large pilgrim crowd moving along, warm daylight. Bartimaeus now without his beggar's cloak, walking tall among them.
- **Camera:** Track with Bartimaeus walking in the crowd behind Yeshua; 85mm on his wide, wonder-filled eyes taking in the world; cut to what he sees — faces, palms, sky; wide/drone pulling up over the crowd streaming down the road with him among them, following.
- **Lyric:** "The prisoners find freedom."

## Scene 23 — Breakfast on the Shore
- **Subject:** Yeshua sharing a morning meal of bread and fish with his disciples on the beach.
- **Action:** A tender, intimate gathering. Yeshua has made a charcoal fire on the shore and cooks fish and bread. The disciples come in from the boat and sit with him in the early light. He hands them bread and fish; they eat together quietly, warmly, in fellowship. Friendship, provision, belonging — the risen friend feeding his own.
- **Scene:** The shore of the Sea of Galilee at dawn, a small charcoal fire glowing, fish and bread, a boat pulled up on the beach, soft golden morning light, calm water, mist lifting.
- **Camera:** Close on the charcoal fire, the fish and bread cooking; widen to Yeshua tending it; the disciples arriving and settling around; 85mm on hands passing bread, faces in the firelight and dawn glow; hold warm and unhurried; a gentle wider shot of the little group on the quiet shore.
- **Lyric:** Later chorus.

## Scene 24 — The Road With Him (Final Chorus)
- **Subject:** Yeshua walking down the road with all his disciples and the healed followers together.
- **Action:** The grand emotional close. Everyone who was touched through the whole song — the healed leper, the widow and her son, the once-paralyzed man, the bleeding woman, Bartimaeus, the disciples — all walk together with Yeshua down the road, a growing joyful company. Faces full of love, gratitude and belonging. They are his, and he is theirs — walking into the light together. The whole song resolves into this family on the move with their Savior.
- **Scene:** An open road through the Galilean countryside into golden light, rolling hills, warm late-day/golden-hour glow, the whole company walking toward the horizon.
- **Camera:** Open on individual faces among the walking company — each one we recognize from earlier scenes; widen to show them all together around Yeshua; move up and behind into a rising drone as the whole group walks down the road toward the glowing horizon, the camera lifting higher and higher until they're a joyful procession moving into the light. The final, soaring shot of the film.
- **Lyric:** Final chorus — "Jesus Savior My Redeemer / Precious Guardian precious Friend."
