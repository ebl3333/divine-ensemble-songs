# Jesus Savior Redeemer Friend — Scenes 1–14 (Detailed)

Album: *God Is About to Show Up* · Divine Ensemble · Production: Dazadi Music

Each scene has: Subject · Action · Scene · Camera · Lyric · Avoid.

**Standard AVOID list (applies to every scene):** no printed words or titles on the image, no black bars, no white edge lines, no collage or grid, no vignette, no color grade, no pink tint (keep it gold), no plastic or overly smooth skin texture, no jarring cuts, no bloody scenes. Jesus kneels only when praying. Generally no film grain — exception noted per scene.

---

## Scene 1 — Toward Jerusalem (Intro)
- **Subject:** Yeshua — shoulder-length dark hair, full beard, cream linen robe and striped prayer shawl — walking alone with a wooden staff, determined, beginning his ministry.
- **Action:** Walking alone toward Jerusalem in the heat; sometimes sitting to rest, sometimes walking on. Sweating, sandalled feet on rocky ground, passing travelers but staying focused. Harsh sun casting long dramatic shadows; a nostalgic, reflective mood.
- **Scene:** Dusty road into Jerusalem, dry desert heat, bright afternoon light, shimmering heat haze rising off the ground throughout.
- **Camera:** Opens on a drone shot following him from above, drifting left and right; then cuts to close-ups — sweat on his face, his staff, his sandalled feet on the rocky ground.
- **Lyric:** Intro (no sung lyric).

## Scene 2 — The Prayer (Intro / prelude)
- **Subject:** Yeshua, alone at night, kneeling in prayer, gazing up at the stars.
- **Action:** On his long journey he endures the nights outside; he kneels in prayer to the Father, lifts his eyes to the stars, talking to heaven.
- **Scene:** Dark, starry night, vast shimmering sky, quiet wilderness. A campfire glows faintly nearby, its warm light sparkling and falling softly on him. Stars twinkle/sparkle.
- **Camera:** 35mm lens. Slow push-in as he kneels, then a smooth crane rise past him toward the twinkling stars — shallow depth of field, firelight warm on his face. Cinematic.
- **Film grain:** Fine film grain allowed here for a cozy, intimate mood.
- **Lyric:** Intro (no sung lyric).

## Scene 3 — The Baptism
- **Subject:** Yeshua and John the Baptist, alone together in the water; the Spirit resting on Yeshua.
- **Action:** Lines of 300–400 people follow John down to the Sea of Galilee and wade in to be baptized, taking turns while others wait on the shore. Yeshua goes down too; he and John move to the middle of the water, away from everyone. The sky gently opens — a soft, warm beam of light (not scary) rests on Yeshua. A dove descends — half translucent, formed of sparkling light, its bird-shape flickering like fire and glinting onto him. Only John sees it. As Yeshua comes up out of the water, shimmering droplets fall and sparkle off him.
- **Scene:** Early afternoon, clear sky, natural river water you can't see through, the Sea of Galilee, crowds along the shore.
- **Camera:** Cinematic wide shot — sweeping drone over the Sea of Galilee, long lines of people and crowded shore, the two men small at the center. Drifts in toward them; as the light comes down, a gentle push-in on Yeshua with a tilt up to follow the light and the dove, settling close on his face.
- **Lyric:** "The Spirit of Jehovah / is resting upon Me."

## Scene 4 — Upon the Waters
- **Subject:** Yeshua walking on the water toward the disciples' boat; four or five disciples in the boat.
- **Action:** At night, strong wind drives rhythmic, choppy waves (not a heavy storm, no rain). Disciples strain in their rocking boat, sail down against the wind, about 100 feet from Yeshua. Peter points at him; others hold up lamps; one grips a pole and raises a light, staring in disbelief; some shrink back in fear, thinking they see a ghost. At the edge of the boat, John sees him. Yeshua — head wrapped in his mantle, cloak drawn around him against the wind — walks calmly across the water toward them.
- **Scene:** Sea of Galilee at night, dark and windy, choppy rhythmic waves, no rain, slivers of moonlight breaking through the clouds, catching the water and spray.
- **Camera:** Cinematic, anamorphic widescreen, shallow depth of field. Opens in a personal POV inside the rocking boat (their fear, the raised light, the wind), then lifts into the air and sweeps behind Yeshua, looking over his back across ~100 feet of water at the boat. Cutaways: close-ups of the disciples' shocked faces, and a close-up of Yeshua's feet stepping across the water.
- **Lyric:** "By Your Spirit we are rising / in Your strength we soar along."

## Scene 5 — The Scroll
- **Subject:** Yeshua, dressed as a humble rabbi, reading the scroll of Isaiah in the synagogue.
- **Action:** He enters humbly and comes forward, standing to read Isaiah (Isaiah 61 / Luke 4) from the scroll — parchment on wooden rollers — then sits in the center to teach. The common people nod and agree; the elegant Pharisees and Sadducees at the front look taken aback; grave grey-bearded elders watch from the honored front seats.
- **Scene:** An authentic, modest first-century Capernaum-style synagogue (Sabbath, daytime). Local basalt stone walls, plain floor with woven mats/rugs, stone benches along the walls (people face inward), a few columns and wooden-beam roof, small high windows, oil lamps, a simple niche/chest for the scrolls. ~150 people in period dress. **Dress:** common people in undyed/earth-toned wool-linen tunics, belts, cloaks, head coverings, sandals; Yeshua humble with tzitzit tassels; Pharisees in finer fuller robes, broad tassels, wider phylacteries, dyed trim.
- **Camera:** 50mm for the room, 85mm for close-ups (people nodding, Pharisees' shocked faces). Reverent, hushed, intimate, holy mood; warm daylight shafts, oil lamps glowing.
- **Lyric:** "I'm anointed by my Father / to bring hope to the weary / to bring love to the lost."

## Scene 6 — The Leper
- **Subject:** Yeshua healing one leper on the wilderness road, his disciples nearby.
- **Action:** A single leper (Matthew 8 / Mark 1) approaches at a distance, keeping back, then falls and begs. Where everyone else pulls back, Yeshua steps forward, reaches out and touches him, and draws back his hood — revealing his face made whole, sores gone, skin clean and new, eyes full of wonder. **Symptoms (kept visible but not gory):** pale/white patches, scaly rough areas, raised sores, discolored blotches.
- **Scene:** Dry, rocky open wilderness — dusty earth, scattered rocks and boulders, pale scrub, barren rolling hills, a worn dirt road. The leper wrapped in tattered, dusty cloth, head covered for shade, face shadowed; barefoot or in broken sandals. Yeshua with 3–4 close disciples; distant travelers/villagers watching warily from afar.
- **Camera:** Wide establishing shot of the vast landscape; move in as the man kneels and begs; tight 85mm close-up on Yeshua's hand touching the marked skin and on the hood coming back; close-up on the man's face/hands as the skin turns whole; pull back as he rises. Warm gold light.
- **Lyric:** "Chains are breaking hearts are rising / as Your freedom is revealed."

## Scene 7 — Rise and Walk
- **Subject:** Yeshua healing the paralyzed man lowered through the roof; his four friends.
- **Action:** The house is jammed wall to wall (Mark 2), no room at the door. The four friends carry their friend up to the roof (outside stone stair/ladder, or stepping onto the flat roof from higher ground at the back on the hillside), scrape off the clay plaster and pull up the reed slabs to open a ragged hole, and lower him on his mat by corner ropes through the shaft of daylight, down to Yeshua's feet.
- **Scene:** Geometrically correct single-storey basalt-stone Capernaum house (walls can't support a second storey). Flat roof — timber beams ~7–8 ft above the floor, ~2 ft apart, covered with branches/reeds then packed clay/mud plaster rolled flat. **The lame man:** thin, legs wasted and limp, simple undyed tunic, worn cloak, anxious but hopeful. **The mat:** a humble reed/straw pallet fraying at the edges, cords tied to its four corners. **Crowd dress:** earth-toned tunics, cloaks, head coverings; scribes/Pharisees in finer robes with visible tassels. Dust and debris drift down in the beam of light.
- **Camera:** Establishing on the packed room and the roof opening against the sky (friends silhouetted); shot of the four carrying him up the outside steps / hillside, effort and determination on their faces; the lowering through the light; tight on the healing.
- **Lyric:** "You have sent me with the promise / for the broken to be healed."

## Scene 8 — The Crowd and the Hem
- **Subject:** Yeshua pressed on all sides by a crowd; the bleeding woman reaching for the hem of his garment.
- **Action:** Yeshua is famous now; people throng toward him in a busy marketplace, squeezing in left and right, reaching to touch him, children darting and reaching, parents lifting little ones. The woman (12 years bleeding — Mark 5 / Matthew 9 / Luke 8), weak and pale in a worn cloak, pushes low through the press and touches his hem. Instantly healed, strength returning to her face. Yeshua stops, feels power go out of him, turns: "Who touched me?" The crowd stills.
- **Scene:** A busy marketplace — stalls, baskets, goods, bustle; a dense, jostling, lively crowd.
- **Camera:** Start high and wide over the market; move down and in through the throng, handheld and alive, weaving between shoulders and reaching hands; follow the woman low at waist height, her hand stretching out; tight 85mm close-up on her fingers touching the hem, then her face as color returns; settle as Yeshua turns. Wide for the market, 85mm for close-ups.
- **Lyric:** "Binding wounds You heal my pain."

## Scene 9 — New Wine (Cana)
- **Subject:** Yeshua turning water into wine at the wedding feast; the servants and the steward.
- **Action:** The wine runs out (a quiet shaming crisis); Mary tells the servants, "Do whatever he tells you." Six tall waist-high stone water jars (20–30 gallons each) stand in a row; servants fill them to the brim with water. As it's drawn out and poured, it has become deep red wine, catching the lamplight. The steward tastes, astonished — the best wine saved for last. Mary watches with quiet knowing; Yeshua calm and unhurried, the miracle almost hidden.
- **Scene:** A joyful first-century Jewish wedding in Cana — a family courtyard ~30×40 ft with surrounding rooms, decorated with hanging lamps, garlands, low tables, cushions and mats. 50–100 guests in festive dress reclining and celebrating; music, dancing, laughter, children. Warm evening light, oil lamps glowing gold; warm and crowded, full of life.
- **Camera:** Wide on the packed glowing courtyard; drift in through the feast toward the quiet edge; settle on the jars and worried servants, then Mary's words; tight 85mm close-up on the pour transforming into deep red wine and the servant's astonished face; pull back out as the gladness spreads and dancing swells.
- **Lyric:** "Pouring gladness over me."

## Scene 10 — The Widow's Son (Nain)
- **Subject:** Yeshua stopping a funeral procession and raising the widow's dead son.
- **Action:** A slow mournful procession carries the dead young man out on an open bier wrapped in burial cloths; mourners weep, hired wailing women and a flute player. The mother — a widow who has already lost her husband and now her only son — is broken with grief. Yeshua is moved with compassion: "Do not weep." He touches the bier; the bearers stop. "Young man, I say to you, arise." The son opens his eyes, sits up, begins to speak. Yeshua gives him back to his mother; her grief turns to astonished joy as she embraces him; the crowd is seized with awe.
- **Scene:** The gate of the town of Nain on a hillside road, dusty open ground outside the town wall, late afternoon warm low light.
- **Camera:** Wide on the town gate — Yeshua's group on one side, the funeral procession coming out on the other, meeting in low golden light; move in on the widow's grief-stricken face; tighten as Yeshua touches the bier and the bearers halt; close on the young man's face as his eyes open and he sits up; cut to the mother breaking from grief into joy; pull back as they embrace and the crowd falls into awe. Wide for the two processions, 85mm for faces.
- **Lyric:** "Beauty rises from the ashes / In Your love You set me free."

## Scene 11 — Bartimaeus Sees
- **Subject:** Yeshua healing blind Bartimaeus, the roadside beggar.
- **Action:** Bartimaeus (Mark 10) sits in the dust at the road's edge; hearing Yeshua pass, he cries "Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!" The crowd tries to hush him; he shouts louder. Yeshua stops: "Call him." Bartimaeus throws off his cloak, springs up, is brought forward. "What do you want me to do for you?" "Rabbi, let me see." "Receive your sight; your faith has healed you." His clouded eyes clear — he blinks into the light, seeing faces, the sky, Yeshua — overwhelmed with joy, and follows him praising God.
- **Scene:** The roadside just outside Jericho — a palm-filled oasis town (few thousand residents), town walls nearby, a dusty road busy with a large crowd (several hundred, Passover pilgrims). A few beggars along the road; Bartimaeus in a worn dusty cloak, begging bowl/outstretched hands, clouded eyes. Warm daylight.
- **Camera:** Wide/drone (24–35mm) for the road, palms, and streaming crowd with Bartimaeus small and low in the dust; move in as he cries out; follow as he throws off his cloak and is brought forward; tight 85mm on his clouded eyes clearing, light filling them, his face breaking into wonder; pull back as he rises and follows.
- **Lyric:** "You declare this is the season / of Your mercy of Your light."

## Scene 12 — Peace Be Still
- **Subject:** Yeshua asleep in the boat during a fierce storm, then rising to still the wind and waves.
- **Action:** A sudden violent night storm (Matthew 8 / Mark 4 / Luke 8). The twelve disciples are terrified, soaked, bailing water, hauling on sail and oars, certain they'll drown. Yeshua is asleep in the stern on a cushion, at peace. They wake him — "Teacher, don't you care that we're perishing?" He rises, stretches out his hand, and speaks: "Peace, be still." Instantly the wind drops, the waves fall, the sea goes glassy. The disciples stare in awe — "Who then is this?"
- **Scene:** The Sea of Galilee at night in a violent storm — black churning water, towering waves, howling wind, the ~27-ft wooden fishing boat tossed and pitching, water crashing over the sides; a few other smaller boats struggling nearby (Mark 4, "other boats were with him"). Slivers of moonlight; storm clouds part at the calm.
- **Camera:** Drone bookends. Open with a sweeping drone high over the storm across the towering waves and struggling boats; drop into handheld chaos pitching with the boat (bailing, shouting); cut to the stillness of Yeshua asleep; follow him rising; as he speaks, the camera settles and steadies; a slow rising drone pan reveals all the boats on glassy mirror-like water under moonlight; close 85mm on the disciples' awe-struck faces. (Distinct from Scene 4: this is the full violent storm with him in the boat.)
- **Lyric:** "The fearful walk in faith."

## Scene 13 — The Bread Shared (Feeding of the 5,000)
- **Subject:** Yeshua blessing and multiplying the loaves and fish to feed the vast crowd.
- **Action:** 5,000 men plus women and children have followed him to a remote place and stayed late (Matthew 14 / Mark 6 / Luke 9 / John 6). A young boy offers his five barley loaves and two small fish; Andrew brings him forward. Yeshua takes them, lifts his eyes to heaven, gives thanks, breaks the bread, and hands it to the disciples, who carry basket after basket to the seated groups — and it never runs out. Twelve baskets of leftovers gathered.
- **Scene:** A remote green hillside near the Sea of Galilee, the lake glinting below. Thousands seated in groups across the slopes, families together. **Time:** late afternoon into early dusk — warm low golden light, long shadows, the lake catching the last light, the glow of approaching evening.
- **Camera:** Open with a sweeping drone over the vast hillside showing the scale of the thousands and the lake; come down to the boy stepping forward, close on his small basket; follow Yeshua taking it, lifting his eyes, breaking the bread (tight on his hands); track the disciples carrying baskets through the seated families, close on their wonder; pull back wide again over the whole hillside eating and satisfied. Wide/drone for scale, 85mm for the boy, the bread, the faces.
- **Lyric:** "Jesus God's Anointed / lifting burdens giving hope."

## Scene 14 — Follow Me (Closing)
- **Subject:** Yeshua on the shore calling his disciples to follow him.
- **Action:** The fishermen — Simon Peter, Andrew, James, John — are at their boats and nets (Mark 1). Yeshua approaches and gives the call, warm and personal: "Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men." They set down their nets and follow, falling into step with him. A sense of new beginning, of being chosen and loved — the emotional close of the whole song.
- **Scene:** The shore of the Sea of Galilee at dawn — golden early light, calm water lapping, fishing boats and nets along the beach, soft hills in the distance. Intimate: the four disciples called, plus one or two background figures at the boats (e.g., Zebedee / hired men). (Bookends Scene 1's heat with fresh morning light.)
- **Camera:** Open low and close on the shore (water, nets, boats, fishermen at work in soft golden light); Yeshua enters and the camera follows him approaching; move in on his face and outstretched hand at the call; cut to the disciples' faces — decision, then resolve — as they set down their nets; follow behind as they fall into step; lift into a slow rising drone as they walk away down the shoreline into the golden light, the sea stretching beside them. 85mm for faces and the call, wide/drone for the closing walk.
- **Lyric:** "Jesus Savior My Redeemer / Precious Guardian precious Friend."
