# Jesus Savior Redeemer Friend

Album: *God Is About to Show Up* · Divine Ensemble · Production: Dazadi Music

The song is Isaiah 61, which Yeshua reads in the synagogue in Luke 4. The video follows Him from Jerusalem and the Jordan through the miracles of Galilee, ending with "Follow Me" on the shore.
