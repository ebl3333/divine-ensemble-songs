Times come from Ed's lyric timing. **H** = human height (eye level). **D** = drone (rises high over the scene). Indoors, D is a high crane from the ceiling.
Every picture is a **starting frame only**: LTX films freely from it, and the "Ends on" column tells LTX where the shot finishes.
**Slow** = instrumental: filmed as slow, gentle motion, then played at about 75% (the long break at about 60%).
