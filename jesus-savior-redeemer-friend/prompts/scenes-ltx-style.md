# Jesus Savior Redeemer Friend — LTX-style clip prompts (all 24 scenes)

Album: *God Is About to Show Up* · Divine Ensemble · Production: Dazadi Music

Every scene from `SCENES-01-14-DETAILED.md` and `SCENES-15-24-DETAILED.md`, rewritten the way LTX works best: one flowing paragraph, present tense, in the order **subject → action → camera → lighting → lens → how the frame ends**.

## How to use

- **One prompt = one clip, 8 seconds or less.** Paste the paragraph and the AVOID line together, with that scene's still as the starting picture.
- **Each scene has two clips, two angles of the same story:** **A** is close, at human height. **B** is wide, from the drone (or a high crane indoors). Whenever a shot in `SHOT-LIST.md` runs longer than 8 seconds, play clip A, then cut to clip B at the start of the second lyric line. Never put a walking shot in the middle of a scene.
- **Walking clips W1–W6** are only for the instrumental breaks.
- **Settings:** 1920×1080, 24 frames per second, prompt enhancer **off** (these prompts are already complete).

## Standing rules for Yeshua (already written into every prompt)

Copy these sentences word for word; never reword them, so his look stays the same in every clip.

- **Look:** *Yeshua, a man in his early thirties with shoulder-length dark hair and a full dark beard, wearing a cream linen robe and a cream prayer shawl with dark blue stripes and tassels, has striking, clear blue-green eyes.*
- **Framing:** *The camera stays close and beside him, in profile or three-quarter view and never directly in front of him, resting on the line of his chin and beard, his mouth, and his clear blue-green eyes as they catch the light.*
- **Wide and drone shots use this instead of the framing line:** *Seen from a distance, he stays small in the frame and is never turned squarely toward the camera.*
- **Outdoors by day, add:** *His prayer shawl is drawn up over his head, softly shadowing his face beneath the cloth.* (Indoors and at night the shawl may be down; the close framing and profile angles keep his face partial.)

## Standard AVOID line (end of every prompt)

> AVOID: printed words or titles, black bars, white edge lines, collage or grid, vignette, color grade, pink tint (keep warm gold), plastic or overly smooth skin, film grain, jarring cuts, blood; Yeshua's face never shown full and head-on; he kneels only when praying.
